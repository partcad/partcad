# Market for online 3D printing / on-demand additive manufacturing services (US and Europe), as of late 2026

Research date: 2026-11-22. Figures are labelled with the period they describe. Where the only source is a secondary or affiliated outlet, that is said beside the claim.

## 1. Market size and growth (US and Europe), by analyst source and methodology

### Takeaway
Services have become the largest and fastest-growing part of additive manufacturing (AM). Wohlers puts global AM services at **$11.7B in 2025 (+15.5%)**, which is 48% of a $24.2B AM market. AM Research's quarterly tracker shows services at **~$2.50B in Q2 2026 (+14% YoY)**, about $9.5B over the trailing four quarters. No source I could reach gives a clean US-only or Europe-only services figure. Wohlers' regional growth rates show the Americas (12.6%) growing faster than EMEA (9.0%), and APAC (19.8%) growing fastest of the three.

### Cited Findings
- **Wohlers Report 2026** (31st edition, released Feb 2026): the global AM market was **$24.2B in 2025, up 10.9% YoY**. That is a slight uptick from 2024 but well below the 20%+ growth seen before the pandemic. — [TCT Magazine](https://www.tctmagazine.com/wohlers-report-2026-additive-manufacturing-revenues-reach-24-2-billion/); [ASTM press release](https://www.astm.org/news/press-releases/wohlers-report-2026)
- Wohlers 2026, breakdown:
  - **Services revenue: $11.7B in 2025, +15.5%**, about **48% of the total market**.
  - Printer sales and servicing: $6.2B (+3.6%).
  - Materials: $4.9B.

  Growth is described as shifting "from machines to services", and value creation as increasingly concentrated in production. — [3D Printing Industry](https://3dprintingindustry.com/news/am-sector-revenue-reaches-24-2bn-as-growth-shifts-from-machines-to-services-249373/); [3Dnatives](https://www.3dnatives.com/en/wohlers-report-2026-17022026/)
- Wohlers 2026, regional growth: companies reported average revenue growth of **19.8% in Asia-Pacific, 12.6% in the Americas and 9.0% in EMEA**. The report is based on more than 300 interviews with AM managers and directors. — [3Dnatives](https://www.3dnatives.com/en/wohlers-report-2026-17022026/); [Wohlers Associates](https://wohlersassociates.com/press-releases/new-wohlers-report-2026-values-additive-manufacturing-market-at-24-2b/) (the primary page returned 403 to my fetch; figures confirmed via trade press)
- Methodology difference: All3DP notes that Wohlers values the market "far higher" than other industry reports such as **AM Research, AMPower and VoxelMatters Research**. — [All3DP](https://all3dp.com/4/the-3d-printing-market-just-hit-24-2b-but-the-growth-is-no-longer-where-you-think/)
- **AM Research**, quarterly figures. It tracks the market by vendor, technology, geography and application, with about 10 years of quarterly history.
  - AM services revenue was about **$2.50B in Q2 2026**, up from $2.19B in Q2 2025 (about +14%). Services are **more than half** of a $4.48B quarterly AM market.
  - Services by quarter: Q1'25 ~$2.18B, Q2'25 ~$2.19B, Q3'25 ~$2.24B, Q4'25 ~$2.32B, Q1'26 ~$2.48B, Q2'26 ~$2.50B.
  - The total AM market grew 12% YoY in Q2 2026, its sixth consecutive quarter of growth.
  - In Q2 2026, polymer AM (all revenue types) was $2.68B and metal AM was $1.81B.

  Source: [3DPrint.com](https://3dprint.com/333786/3d-printing-services-near-2-5b-as-am-market-shifts-beyond-hardware/amp/), about Sept 2026.
- **"Service bureau" market estimates from generalist market-research firms disagree widely**:
  - Spherical Insights: ~$7.1B in 2024, rising to $45.5B by 2035 (18.4% CAGR), with North America at a 38% share. — [Spherical Insights](https://www.sphericalinsights.com/press-release/global-3d-printing-service-bureau-market)
  - Verified Market Research: $5.3B in 2024. — [Verified Market Research](https://www.verifiedmarketresearch.com/product/3d-print-service-bureau-market/)
  - Persistence Market Research: North America held a 28.5% share in 2022. — [Persistence](https://www.persistencemarketresearch.com/mediarelease/3d-printing-service-bureaus-market.asp)
  - The Business Research Company: APAC was the largest region in 3D printing services in 2025, with North America second. — [GII/TBRC](https://giiresearch.com/report/tbrc1712274-3d-printing-services-global-market-report.html)
- **Grand View Research** publishes a *total* 3D printing market figure, not a services one: **$168.93B by 2033**. That scope is far broader than Wohlers or AM Research. — [Grand View Research](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/press-release/global-3d-printing-market)

### Inferences
- The trailing four quarters of AM Research data (Q3'25 to Q2'26) sum to about **$9.5B** of services, compared with Wohlers' $11.7B for calendar 2025. The ~20% gap is consistent with All3DP's remark that Wohlers counts more broadly. The ordering decision should rely on relative trends, which are robust across sources: services are growing at roughly 14–15%/yr, 3–4x faster than hardware. It should not rely on absolute numbers.
- The Spherical, Verified and Persistence figures ($5–7B and 18% CAGRs) use undisclosed definitions and are not comparable with Wohlers or AM Research. Treat them as low-quality.
- On Wohlers' growth rates, Europe (EMEA) is the slowest-growing AM region. On the online-ordering side, Xometry's US revenue is also growing faster than its international revenue (see section 2). Both point to the **US as the larger and faster-growing market for API- or online-ordered parts**.
- The services that matter for PartCAD's use case (online instant-quote ordering of prototypes and short runs) are a subset of "AM services". Most Wohlers or AM Research services revenue is industrial contract production, often negotiated offline.

### Gaps
- No reachable source gives a **US-only or Europe-only 3D printing services market size** for 2025–2026. Wohlers gives growth by region but, in the press coverage, no regional revenue split. AMPower (Europe-focused) and VoxelMatters Research figures were not found.
- Grand View's services-specific report (if one exists) and SmarTech/AM Research annual services totals were not reachable.
- No source sizes the "online instant-quote" sub-segment (Xometry-, Protolabs-, Craftcloud- or JLC-style ordering) separately from industrial contract manufacturing.

## 2. Leading players: revenue, share, trajectory

### Takeaway
**Xometry is by far the largest and fastest-growing online manufacturing marketplace.** It reported a ~$1B revenue run-rate, 40% YoY growth and its first GAAP profit in Q3 2026. **Protolabs is the second Western public player** at ~$154M per quarter, but 3D printing is only ~15% of its revenue. **Materialise's Manufacturing segment is roughly flat** at ~€47M per half-year. Among private and mid-size players: Fictiv is now owned by MISUMI; Sculpteo merged into France's 3D Prod in 2026 after BASF/Forward AM turmoil; Shapeways is a small relaunched business pivoting to creator print-on-demand (Thangs); Craftcloud (All3DP's parent company) is a ~200-supplier price-comparison marketplace.

## 3. Consolidation, failures and exits 2022–2026: what they imply for counterparty risk

### Takeaway
2024–2025 brought a wave of failures and consolidation across AM hardware and services:
- Shapeways went bankrupt (Chapter 7) and was relaunched.
- Forward AM went insolvent and Stratasys bought its assets.
- Nano Dimension bought Desktop Metal and Markforged; Desktop Metal then went into Chapter 11, and Arc Impact bought its core assets.
- MISUMI bought Fictiv.
- TRUMPF and Arburg exited AM.
- Materialise folded i.materialise into a single platform.

The pattern: **pure-play or VC-funded 3D printing companies failed or were absorbed, while diversified, multi-process, profitable platforms (Xometry, Protolabs, MISUMI/Fictiv) consolidated their position.**

### Cited Findings
- **Desktop Metal:** Nano Dimension completed its acquisition in April 2025 for **$179.3M** (~7% of Desktop Metal's former SPAC-era valuation). Desktop Metal **filed Chapter 11 in late July 2025** (S.D. Texas). — [All3DP](https://all3dp.com/6/is-this-finally-the-end-of-desktop-metal-the-storied-3d-printer-maker-files-for-chapter-11/); [IndustryWeek](https://industryweek.com/technology-and-iiot/additive/news/55306861/nano-dimension-pushes-desktop-metal-through-bankruptcy); [3Dnatives](https://www.3dnatives.com/en/desktop-metal-files-for-chapter-11-bankruptcy-3007202501/)
- **Arc Impact acquired Desktop Metal's core assets** (including Adaptive3D and EnvisionTEC) in Sept 2025. — [3DPrint.com 2025 review](https://3dprint.com/322764/who-merged-who-was-acquired-and-who-disappeared-in-3d-printing-in-2025/)
- **Markforged:** Nano Dimension closed its **$116M** acquisition on April 25, 2025. Markforged's 2024 revenue was >$85M. — [All3DP](https://all3dp.com/6/is-this-finally-the-end-of-desktop-metal-the-storied-3d-printer-maker-files-for-chapter-11/)
- Nano Dimension also sold DeepCube and Formatec and discontinued Fabrica and Admatec. Other 2025 events:
  - TRUMPF sold its AM business to DUBAG Group (July 2025; later reorganised as ATLIX).
  - Arburg exited AM (Sept 2025).
  - BCN3D filed for bankruptcy (June 2025).
  - ExOne and voxeljet were combined under one holding company.
  - Castor Technologies (AM decision software) was liquidated (July 2025).
  - Aniwaa shut down (April 2025).

  Source: [3DPrint.com 2025 review](https://3dprint.com/322764/who-merged-who-was-acquired-and-who-disappeared-in-3d-printing-in-2025/)
- Analysts describe 2025 as closing "the chapter on the first generation of publicly traded AM startups". — [3DPrint.com 2025 review](https://3dprint.com/322764/who-merged-who-was-acquired-and-who-disappeared-in-3d-printing-in-2025/)
- **Shapeways:** Chapter 7 in July 2024, relaunched by its founders in Dec 2024. — [3DPrint.com](https://3dprint.com/314952/shapeways-relaunched-with-all-assets-under-one-roof/?noamp=mobile); [Develop3D](https://develop3d.com/3d-printing/shapeways-3d-printing-service-returns-in-hands-of-original-founders/)
- **Forward AM / Sculpteo:** see section 2 (insolvency Nov 2024, assets to Stratasys, Sculpteo to 3D Prod in May 2026).
- **Velo3D** (metal printer maker): headlines report revenue up 52%, a raised 2026 forecast and a new Livermore production site. These are **undated search snippets and not verified**. — [3DPrint.com tag pages via search](https://3dprint.com/tag/slant/)

### Inferences
- **Counterparty-risk tiers for an API integration** (my synthesis):
  - **Low:** Xometry (public, GAAP-profitable, $1B run-rate); Protolabs (public, profitable, but has two ordering front-ends); Fictiv (owned by MISUMI).
  - **Medium:** Materialise (public, but the Manufacturing segment is flat and its online platform was consolidated in 2025); Craftcloud (private, a media-company subsidiary; aggregation lowers dependence on any single supplier); JLC3DP (very large parent, but exposed to tariffs and policy).
  - **Higher:** Shapeways (post-bankruptcy, small, strategy shifting toward creator print-on-demand); Sculpteo/3D Prod (just merged, platform future unknown); Slant 3D and other single-company print farms (private, little financial disclosure).
- History shows that **brand, URL and API continuity is often the casualty even when a business survives**: 3D Hubs became Protolabs Network, i.materialise was merged, and Shapeways' old API customers went through the bankruptcy. An integration layer in PartCAD should therefore be **provider-pluggable**, so that a failed or renamed backend is a configuration change rather than a code rewrite.
- Aggregators and marketplaces (Xometry, Craftcloud, Protolabs Network) spread supplier failure risk across many shops. Single-factory services concentrate it.

### Gaps
- **Fast Radius** (Chapter 11 in late 2022, assets reportedly sold to SyBridge) and **Voodoo Manufacturing** (closure): not verified in this session, so both are excluded from the cited findings.
- **Stratasys' current financial position** and Stratasys Direct's status were not researched.
- Whether Nano Dimension still holds Markforged's service offerings in 2026 was not verified.

## 4. Customer segments, which services serve them, and price levels

### Takeaway
The market splits three ways:
- **Hobbyist / maker / creator:** Craftcloud, Shapeways/Thangs print-on-demand, JLC3DP and PCBWay, Treatstock, Slant 3D for product sellers.
- **Engineering prototyping and SME:** Xometry, Protolabs/Protolabs Network, Fictiv, Craftcloud, Weerg, Sculpteo.
- **Production / industrial** (aerospace, defense, medical): Materialise Manufacturing, Protolabs, Xometry enterprise accounts, Stratasys Direct.

The only published cross-service price benchmarks come from All3DP, which shares a parent company with Craftcloud. They show Craftcloud roughly half the price of Xometry and well below Protolabs Network for a sample part.

- For PartCAD's users (engineers and makers writing CAD-as-code), the most relevant segments are **prototyping/SME** (Xometry, Protolabs, Craftcloud, Fictiv) and **maker/hobbyist** (Craftcloud, JLC3DP, Shapeways, Slant 3D).

### Gaps

- JLC3DP and PCBWay **US/EU market penetration** among hobbyists and SMEs: no quantitative data found (revenue, order counts or share).
- No source gives segment mix (hobbyist / prototype / production) for any provider.

## 5. Trends: API ordering, AI quoting, CAD-integrated ordering, distributed networks, tariffs, EU regulation

### Takeaway
- **Instant online quoting is now table stakes**, and Xometry now markets itself as "AI-native".
- **CAD-integrated "get a quote" buttons exist mainly from Xometry** (SOLIDWORKS, Autodesk Fusion, Onshape, Inventor).
- **Public ordering APIs are rare**: Slant 3D is a notable example, and Craftcloud and Shapeways have historically offered APIs.
- **US tariffs since 2025 materially raise the cost of Chinese services for US buyers.** JLC3DP pre-collects 40% on plastic parts and 70% on 316L steel parts as of March 2026, and has forced US individuals under $800 onto prepaid-duty (DDP) shipping since May 2, 2025.

### Cited Findings
- **AI quoting:** Xometry calls itself an "AI-native marketplace" and introduced new **AI sourcing models in July 2026**. — [FinancialContent release](https://www.financialcontent.com/article/gnwcq-2026-11-3-xometry-reports-record-third-quarter-2026-results)
- **CAD-integrated ordering:**
  - Xometry offers free add-ins for **SOLIDWORKS, Autodesk Fusion and Onshape** (via the Onshape App Store), plus a page for **Inventor**.
  - The add-ins give an instant price and design-for-manufacturing feedback inside the CAD tool; the order is then completed on xometry.com.
  - The Fusion add-in is Windows-only, and its prices are for North America only (reference-only elsewhere). Uploads are capped at 100 MB.

  Source: [Xometry CAD add-ins](https://xometry.com/cad-add-ins); [Xometry Onshape app blog](https://www.xometry.com/resources/blog/introducing-the-xometry-onshape-app); [Xometry community KB](https://community.xometry.com/kb/categories/242-xometry-cad-add-ins); [Digital Engineering](https://digitalengineering247.com/article/xometry-instant-pricing-app-accessible-in-the-onshape-store)
- **Programmatic ordering:** Slant 3D runs a public API (beta from 2023, pay-per-part, routes to the nearest farm, built for connecting e-commerce storefronts). — [TCT Magazine](https://www.tctmagazine.com/slant-3d-api-connect-3d-print-farm-online-marketplace/); [Fabbaloo](https://www.fabbaloo.com/news/slant-3d-launches-api-for-direct-access-to-print-farms)
- **Distributed networks / marketplaces:**
  - Craftcloud aggregates ~200 manufacturers worldwide, with a lowest-global or lowest-local price option. — [All3DP](https://all3dp.com/2/best-cheap-3d-printing-services/)
  - Protolabs runs Protolabs Network (ex-Hubs) alongside its own factories. — [Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20240108659605/en)
  - Xometry's marketplace model is supplier-network based. — [FinancialContent release](https://www.financialcontent.com/article/gnwcq-2026-11-3-xometry-reports-record-third-quarter-2026-results)
- **Tariffs and US buyers of Chinese services (JLC3DP FAQ, updated Mar 19, 2026):**
  - Since **May 2, 2025**, US *individual* customers with orders under $800 must use **DDP** (taxes pre-collected). Company customers may choose CPT (buyer clears customs).
  - JLC3DP gives these tariff dates:
    - June 4, 2025: Section 232 steel/aluminium tariff increase.
    - Nov 11, 2025: change to the additional tariff.
    - **Feb 24, 2026: reciprocal and fentanyl-related tariffs expired**, and a new 10% provisional tariff took effect.
  - **Pre-collected DDP rates from Mar 17, 2026** (described as an "overall 10% reduction"):
    - **Resin, nylon, PA12-CF, ABS, ASA, PLA, TPU: 40%**
    - **316L / BJ-316L stainless: 70%**
    - **Titanium TC4: 35%**
  - The page also cites "175%" and "55–110%" figures that conflict with its own rate table.

  Source: [JLC3DP US tariff FAQ](https://jlc3dp.com/help/article/us-tariff-policy-faq); [JLCPCB tariff updates](https://jlcpcb.com/news/logistics-update-us-china-tariff)
- Forum posts (unofficial) say the US $800 de minimis exemption ended in 2025 and that a 2025 law repeals its statutory basis from July 2027. — [PedalPCB forum](https://forum.pedalpcb.com/goto/post?id=369679) (user discussion; unverified)

### Inferences
- Even after the Feb 2026 tariff relief, a 40% duty uplift on plastic parts erodes much of JLC3DP's price advantage for US buyers on small orders. Shipping and delays add to that. This favours US-domestic networks (Xometry, Protolabs, Craftcloud's US suppliers, Slant 3D) for US PartCAD users. **EU buyers are not subject to these US tariffs**, so JLC3DP/PCBWay likely remain highly price-competitive in Europe. (No EU-side data was found to confirm this.)
- Because the tariff regime has changed repeatedly (May 2025, June 2025, Nov 2025, Feb 2026, Mar 2026), any integration that shows landed cost must take duties from the provider's quote at order time, not hard-code them.
- Xometry's head start on CAD add-ins (Onshape, Fusion, SOLIDWORKS) plus its scale make it the de facto "buy button" incumbent in mainstream CAD. A code-CAD platform integrating with Xometry would be following an established pattern.

### Gaps
- **EU regulation** affecting online manufacturing services (for example GPSR product safety, the EU Customs Reform ending the €150 duty exemption, CBAM, NIS2) was not researched in this session. No source found.
- **Protolabs, Craftcloud, Materialise and Shapeways CAD add-ins:** none found in this search. Whether Protolabs or Fictiv offer Onshape or Fusion apps in 2026 is unverified.
- Whether Xometry or Protolabs expose a **public ordering API** (as opposed to CAD add-ins and web upload) was not established here; other researchers cover APIs.
- No data on the share of orders placed via API or CAD plugin versus web upload, for any provider.

## 6. Ecosystem evidence: integrations in CAD and open-source tools

### Takeaway
The clearest evidence of an ecosystem is **Xometry's add-ins for SOLIDWORKS, Autodesk Fusion, Onshape and Inventor**. Slant 3D's API is aimed at e-commerce and storefront integration, not CAD. I found no evidence in this session of print-service integrations in open-source CAD tools (FreeCAD, OpenSCAD, CadQuery, build123d).

### Cited Findings
- Xometry add-ins: SOLIDWORKS (MSI installer; supported versions are listed inconsistently as 2018–2025 actively updated, or 2022+), Autodesk Fusion (Windows), Onshape (App Store, all plans up to Professional), Inventor. All are free. — [Xometry CAD add-ins](https://xometry.com/cad-add-ins); [Xometry SOLIDWORKS](https://www.xometry.com/solidworks-add-in/); [Xometry Inventor](https://www.xometry.com/cad-add-in-for-inventor); [Xometry community KB](https://community.xometry.com/kb/articles/721-xometry-app-for-onshape-faqs)
- Slant 3D API: positioned for connecting print farms to online storefronts and marketplaces. — [TCT Magazine](https://www.tctmagazine.com/slant-3d-api-connect-3d-print-farm-online-marketplace/)
- Craftcloud is embedded as a "Powered by" quoting widget on trade-media sites (for example 3DPrint.com). This is evidence of embeddable distribution, though not of CAD integration. — [3DPrint.com 2025 review page](https://3dprint.com/322764/who-merged-who-was-acquired-and-who-disappeared-in-3d-printing-in-2025/)

### Inferences
- No open-source code-CAD tool appears to have a first-class "order a print" integration yet. If that holds, PartCAD would be early rather than late. Verify this with the API researchers before stating it in the report.
- A first integration target follows from the evidence above:
  - **Xometry** for the largest, durable, US-centric, AI-quoted marketplace.
  - **Craftcloud** for cheap, multi-supplier, US+EU maker pricing.
  - Possibly **JLC3DP** for EU and price-sensitive users, with tariff caveats for US users.
  - Protolabs is durable but has a split storefront and higher prices. Shapeways and Sculpteo carry the highest durability risk.

### Gaps
- I did not search for FreeCAD, OpenSCAD, Blender, KiCad, CadQuery or build123d plugins for print services (for example, a FreeCAD Craftcloud or Shapeways macro). These may exist and should be checked.
- No usage statistics (installs, orders) for any CAD add-in were found.
